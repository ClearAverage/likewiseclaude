Privacy policy

Your secret stays yours.

Plain-English version. Last updated October 2026.

The short version: your pick is sealed by the database itself. The only person who can ever see it is the person you picked, and only if they picked you too. Every class is locked and wiped 3 days after its reveal.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication (Google) and stored hashed. Likewise never sees your password.
  • Your profile: the display name you choose.
  • Your classes: which classes you’re in, the class name, the host, and the reveal date.
  • Your pick: who you chose in each class, and when.
  • Reports you send, if any.
  • Basic analytics: anonymous page views through Google Analytics, so we know if the site is working. We never send names, classes or picks to analytics.

Who can see what

  • Classmates in the same class can see your display name in the class list. That’s it.
  • Your pick can’t be read by classmates, the host, or any other account. The one exception: after the reveal, if the person you picked also picked you, the two of you can each see that it’s mutual.
  • If it isn’t mutual, nobody learns anything. Not the person you picked, not anyone else.
  • The host has no extra access. They can start the class and remove people from the lobby before it starts.
  • Site administrators. To be fully honest: like every website owner, the people who run this Likewise site can technically open the database in Google’s admin console. The app itself never shows picks to anyone, admins included, and picks are deleted 3 days after each reveal.

How long we keep it

  • Class data (the roster and everyone’s picks) is locked 3 days after the reveal, so nobody can read it anymore, and then deleted.
  • Your account stays until you delete it from the Account page.
  • Reports are kept only as long as moderators need them.

Your controls

You can change your name, email and password, or delete your account at any time from the Account page in the app. Deleting your account removes your profile and any picks that can still be changed.

How it’s protected

  • Server-side security rules decide every read and write. A browser, script or modified app can’t get around them.
  • Email verification is required before you can join or host a class.
  • The reveal date can’t be changed by anyone after a class is created.
  • Picks freeze at the reveal, so nobody can switch picks to test who liked them.
  • Nothing about your picks is saved to your device’s storage.

Age

Likewise is for people 13 and older. If you’re under 13, please don’t create an account.

Questions

Questions about your data? Contact the people who run this Likewise site.
