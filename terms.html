Terms & safety

Be real. Be kind.

Last updated October 2026.

Likewise exists to make one awkward thing a little easier. Using it means agreeing to these rules. They’re short, so please actually read them.

Who can use Likewise

  • You must be 13 or older.
  • One account per person, with an email you can actually check.

Be yourself

  • Use the name your classmates actually know you by.
  • Never pretend to be someone else, and never join a class you don’t belong to.
  • Hosts: before pressing Start, check every name in the lobby and remove anyone you don’t recognize.

Be kind

  • Don’t pressure anyone to tell you who they picked, or to show you their results.
  • Don’t screenshot or share someone else’s result to embarrass them.
  • A match means you both like each other. It isn’t permission for anything. Talk, be respectful, and take “no” as a full answer.
  • “No mutual match” isn’t a judgment of you as a person. It just wasn’t this round.

Don’t try to break it

Likewise is built so that picks can’t be read, changed or guessed. Attempts to get around that, to spam classes, or to mess with other people’s accounts can get your account disabled.

Reports & moderation

Every class has a “Report a problem” button. Reports go privately to the moderators of this site, who may remove classes or disable accounts that break these rules.

If something feels wrong

If anyone is bullying you, pressuring you, or making you feel unsafe, on Likewise or anywhere else, tell a trusted adult like a parent, teacher or school counselor. You don’t have to handle it alone.

The fine print

Likewise is provided as-is, for fun, by the people who run this site. We work hard to keep it secure and online, but we can’t promise it will always be available or error-free. We may update these terms. If we do, the date at the top changes.
